You can edit and delete journals, tasks, and goals, and remove uploaded images. Mawj includes no advertising or analytics trackers.

More technical detail

The operator tool lists usernames and registration dates only; it has no control for opening another account’s content. Hosting operators need technical access to maintain the server and backups, and that access can make data technically available when necessary.
